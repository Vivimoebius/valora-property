Founder & Owner

Vivian Möbius

The strategic layer above your property administration.

Behind Valora stands a promise that Vivian Möbius keeps personally: your property gains someone who thinks ahead, plans ahead and keeps the overview for you – with time for the individual mandate instead of administration on a conveyor belt.

„Good property administration solves problems. Strategic management prevents them.“

Valora Property Advisory was founded by Vivian Möbius. Her path began in traditional property administration – and it was precisely there that the limits became clear: too often the response is merely reactive instead of steered, too often the view of the whole is missing.

She decided to do it differently: fewer mandates, but genuine responsibility for each one. Thinking ahead instead of merely working through a list. And a counterpart who is reachable and speaks plainly – rather than hiding behind reference numbers and forms.

Our clients are owners, investors and entrepreneurs who have high standards but little time. What they receive from us: transparency, value preservation and the certainty that someone is thinking alongside them.

Our Approach

Personal, Not Standard

We do not offer off-the-shelf solutions. Instead, we develop tailored concepts designed around your individual needs and goals.

With Vivian Möbius you have no ticketing system and no rotating case handlers, but one dedicated point of contact who knows your property and keeps your goals in mind. Decisions are prepared, not deferred – and nothing happens over your head.

This closeness is only possible because the number of mandates is deliberately kept small. That is exactly the difference owners feel day to day: fast answers, honest assessments and the good feeling that someone is thinking alongside them.
