The principle

„Don't administer. Steer.
Not standard. Individual.“

Valora is the strategic layer above your property management: we act as the higher-level interface between owner, management and service providers – location-independent, whether in Leipzig, on Palma de Mallorca or anywhere else.

Our promise

We are not a property management company.

We close the gap between owner and management – and stand up for you where you cannot be yourself.

As an independent authority at your side, we take on individual tasks or entire mandates in exclusive care – fast, reliable and always in your interest.

There for you

Exclusive

Fast & reliable

Management instead of administration

Classic property management companies administer. We steer. The difference: we think along with you, optimise structures and create transparency – as a strategic partner on equal footing.

Founded by Vivian Möbius, who knows the weak points of classic management from her own practice – and developed from it an approach that deliberately focuses on a few, intensively supported mandates.

Location-independent. Personal.

Whether a capital investment in Leipzig, a holiday property on Mallorca or an investment in another location – we look after your property wherever it stands. You determine the place, we deliver the strategy.

On Mallorca we work with dedicated local contacts on site – for the complete handling around your property. As a full service on request: key holding, coordination of tradespeople and service providers, regular property inspections and care – especially when you are not on site yourself.

Our clients are owners, investors and entrepreneurs who value economically sound steering and individual solutions.

Service 01

Strategic Property Management

A property management company handles the operational work. We sit one level above – as an independent authority that mediates between you and all parties and holds everything together. You have a single contact instead of many loose threads.

  • Coordination of management, service providers, tradespeople and authorities
  • Preparation and pre-review of decisions – in your interest
  • Ongoing control of costs, quality and deadlines
  • Clear reporting: you always know where you stand

This way your property is not only administered, but actively led.
